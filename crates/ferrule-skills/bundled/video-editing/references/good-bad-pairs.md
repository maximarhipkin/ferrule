# Good and bad, side by side

Calibration: the same moment done badly and well. When you judge a
version, ask which side of each pair it is on.

**Opening a short**
- Bad: a 2-second logo animation, then "Hi everyone, today I want to talk
  about…".
- Good: the result first ("This cost me $40 and saved 6 hours"), then how.

**A cut in speech**
- Bad: the cut lands mid-word ("…we deci|ded"), or a breath and a click
  are left at the joint.
- Good: the cut sits in the pause after the word, padded a little, the
  sound faded 30 ms; the next line starts cleanly.

**Jump cuts**
- Bad: ten cuts of the same framing, each a visible twitch.
- Good: every other segment pushed in to about 110%, or covered with
  B-roll, so each cut reads as a choice.

**Captions on a short**
- Bad: two full lines of small white text at the very bottom, under the
  app's buttons, each up for half a second.
- Good: two or three words at a time, large, outlined, above the covered
  bottom, timed to the words.

**A slide**
- Bad: a paragraph in three sizes and two fonts, left on screen for 2 s.
- Good: a five-word headline, one supporting line, the same type scale as
  every other slide, up until the voice moves on.

**Transitions**
- Bad: a different flashy transition at every cut (spin, cube, page curl).
- Good: plain cuts, and one soft crossfade where time passes.

**Music under a voice**
- Bad: the music at full level the whole way, the voice fighting it; the
  track cut off mid-bar at the end.
- Good: music ducked under speech, up between sentences, ending on a
  phrase or fading over a second.

**Loudness**
- Bad: -22 LUFS (the viewer turns the phone up, then the next video
  blasts), or peaks clipping at +1 dBTP.
- Good: -14 LUFS, true peak under -1 dBTP: `check_video` shows it.

**Generated clips in a promo**
- Bad: a hand with six fingers holding the product; the label's lettering
  garbled; the actor's face changes between shots.
- Good: shots chosen or regenerated until hands, faces and the product
  are right; all text added in the edit.

**Ending**
- Bad: the video just stops mid-thought, or fades to black for 3 s.
- Good: a last line or a payoff, then a clean cut (or a held final frame
  with the call to action long enough to read).
