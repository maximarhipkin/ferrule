# Short vertical clips (Reels, TikTok, Shorts)

`check_video` profile: `short`.

## Shape

- 1080×1920 (9:16), 30 fps, H.264 + AAC, yuv420p, faststart.
- YouTube Shorts take up to 3 minutes; most clips work best far shorter.
  Platforms change their limits; the brief's length wins.
- Keep text and faces in the central area. The apps cover the bottom
  (caption, buttons) and the right edge (likes, share) and some of the top:
  a centre of about 900×1400 is clear on all three (third-party figures,
  not official).

## Structure

1. **Hook, 0–3 s.** The most interesting moment or line first; the
   context after. Start on movement or speech, never on a logo or black.
2. **Body.** One idea. Each beat a few seconds; change the picture when
   the thought changes (a closer shot, B-roll, a caption emphasis).
3. **End.** A payoff or a call to action, then cut. A clean last frame
   makes a loop feel intended.

## Cutting talking footage

- Cut on the timed transcript: remove ums, false starts, repeated takes
  and long pauses. A pause of about 0.4 s or more cuts cleanly; under
  about 0.15 s, a cut tends to clip a sound.
- Never cut inside a word. Pad each cut 30–200 ms into the silence, and
  fade the sound 30 ms at every cut so it doesn't click.
- A jump cut (same framing, a moment later) is fine when it removes dead
  time; hide repeated ones with a small zoom change (scale to 110%, crop
  centre) every other cut, or with B-roll.
- `check_video` warns when one shot holds 8 s or more in a short: that is
  this milestone's rule of thumb, not a platform rule. A hold that earns
  its time can stay.

## Captions

- Most people watch muted: caption all speech, burned in.
- Short chunks (one to four words) timed to the words, each up at least
  0.35 s; large, bold, outlined; in the central area, lower third above
  the covered bottom.
- Emphasise one word at a time at most (colour or size), and only words
  that matter.

## Sound

- Master to -14 LUFS integrated, true peak under -1 dBTP (`sound.md`).
- Music under speech ducked; music alone can carry a montage.
